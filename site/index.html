What This Is

Scoring Standard

Rubric

Fixed scaleVersion 2026-06-strict-standup-v4. 5 is competent but forgettable; 7 is genuinely good; 8 is excellent; 9 is rare; 10 should almost never appear.
  • Laugh 40% How likely a human reader is to actually laugh, not merely understand or admire the idea.
  • Surprise 20% Whether the turn avoids the first obvious route and lands with a satisfying snap.
  • Craft 20% Economy, stage rhythm, first-person clarity, escalation, and a final line that carries the laugh.
  • Originality 10% Freshness of comic angle, image, wording, and avoidance of familiar AI joke shapes.
  • Prompt Fit 10% Natural first-person stand-up form using exactly two seed terms in the joke text, with the other four left out.
  1. 1-2 Broken Not a joke, incoherent, unsafe, or unusable.
  2. 3-4 Weak Recognizably attempting humor, but generic, strained, confusing, or mostly premise recital.
  3. 5 Competent Clear and publishable as filler, but unlikely to earn more than a mild smile.
  4. 6 Amusing A real comic idea with a mild payoff; respectable, not a winner.
  5. 7 Good A genuinely good joke with clear timing; some humans would repeat the premise or turn.
  6. 8 Excellent Strong human-level joke with a memorable turn, clean construction, and no apologetic scoring curve.
  7. 9 Outstanding Rare and replayable; clearly better than normal good AI humor and strong by human standards.
  8. 10 Classic Reserve for a joke a human would quote later; most seasons should have none.

Joke A ChatGPT 6.6

Consent Cardio

I set up at the Park offering free leg stretches to joggers, because I thought, “Everybody looks tense.” It was a Foolish plan. The first guy I approached started sprinting, so I sprinted after him yelling, “I can fix that hamstring!” That’s when I learned wellness becomes a crime at about twelve miles an hour.

  • Claude: Solid escalation and the 'twelve miles an hour' line is a genuine payoff with good specificity. The setup is a touch slow but the punchline earns its place.
  • Gemini: Good escalation. Clear punchline pacing.
Joke B Claude 6.0

Goose Protocol

I'm a massage therapist who sets up in the Park on weekends. Sounds peaceful — it is not. Last Sunday I spent forty minutes herding a client's hamstring back where it belongs, and another twenty explaining to a golden retriever that the table is not a buffet. The dog tipped better.

  • ChatGPT: Clear stage setup and a workable dog tag, but the hamstring-herding image is slightly blurry and the final line feels familiar.
  • Gemini: Competent structure. Standard misdirection punchline.
Joke C Gemini 6.8

Collie Instincts

I took my new rescue collie to the local park. He absolutely refuses to fetch. His whole life revolves around herding. It sounds adorable, right up until I have to explain to a furious mother why her four toddlers are seamlessly corralled into a tight pentagon by the swingset.

  • ChatGPT: Best of the set: clean premise, strong escalation, and the toddlers-in-a-pentagon image gives it a satisfying final laugh.
  • Claude: The pentagon detail is the joke's engine — specific, visual, and unexpected. Doesn't need the premise context to work, which is the mark of a clean standalone.
Joke D Grok 4.7

Parkside Therapy

Folks, I work as a Massage / Physical Therapist at the Park, and let me tell you it's not the zen paradise I pictured. Last week this stressed executive lies down on my portable table and right as I hit the knot in his back, a frisbee smacks him in the head. He bolts up yelling, and I just shrug and say, 'That's nature's way of saying you need deeper work.'

  • ChatGPT: The seed phrase is awkwardly inserted, and the frisbee/deeper-work punchline feels like a predictable restatement of the setup.
  • Claude: Frisbee escalation is functional but the punchline is a therapist-speak callback that sounds like a closer without quite closing.
Joke E Copilot 5.7

Hands Full

As a Massage / Physical Therapist, I never expected my day to turn into full-on Herding. One client brought three friends who all lined up behind me like I was leading a wellness parade. I tried to stay professional, but nothing kills your authority faster than realizing you’re basically the world’s calmest sheepdog with a folding table.

  • ChatGPT: A coherent and mildly funny image with decent wording, though the setup is a bit premise-direct and the sheepdog turn is more cute than surprising.
  • Claude: World's calmest sheepdog with a folding table is a genuinely good image and saves the joke, though the setup is slightly generic.
